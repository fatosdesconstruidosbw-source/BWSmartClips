## English — en-US

**Product name:** BWSmartClips

**Short description:**
Turn long videos into vertical clips ready for Reels, Shorts and TikTok. BWSmartClips transcribes speech, uses AI to find the best moments, reframes to 9:16 following the speaker's face and adds animated captions. Everything runs on your computer: no subscription, no credits and no uploading your videos to the cloud.

**Full description:**
BWSmartClips is an AI clip editor that runs 100% on your PC.

• AI clipping: a local AI reads the transcript and picks the most promising moments, with a virality score and suggested title and hashtags.
• Animated captions: ready-made styles (karaoke, impact, box, neon and more) with the spoken word highlighted.
• 9:16 reframing: follow the speaker's face, fill, blurred background or original format.
• Gameplay layout: for game streams, webcam on top and gameplay below, with automatic webcam detection.
• Local AI, no cloud: transcription (Whisper) and clip selection (Qwen) run on your computer. AMD, NVIDIA or Intel Arc graphics speed up the analysis.
• One-time purchase: buy once, use forever, no subscription.
• Privacy: your videos never leave your PC. No telemetry, no ads.

Requirements: Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit; 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended); about 5 GB free for the AI models, downloaded on first launch. A graphics card is optional.

Generative AI use: titles, scores and hashtags are generated by a local AI and may contain mistakes; review them before publishing. You can report inappropriate content inside the app (Settings → About).

**Features:**
- Automatic clips with local artificial intelligence
- Animated captions in several styles
- 9:16 reframing that follows the face
- Gameplay layout with webcam on top
- Virality score, suggested title and hashtags
- Works offline, no subscription
- Optional GPU acceleration

**Keywords:** video clips, shorts, reels, tiktok, captions, podcast, video editor

**What's new:** First release on the Microsoft Store.
