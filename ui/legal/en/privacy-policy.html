This policy explains which data the BWSmartClips app ("app") handles, why, and with whom it may be shared. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law 13,709/2018, "LGPD") and the Microsoft Store policies.

1. Summary

  • BWSmartClips processes your videos on your own computer. Transcription, clip selection by artificial intelligence (AI), reframing and captions run locally.
  • We do not receive your videos, transcripts, clips, settings or usage data. The app has no telemetry, advertising or tracking.
  • The app only connects to the internet in the situations described in section 4. Some of them require you to turn them on.

2. Data handled on your computer

The following stays only on your computer, in the app folders and in the clips folder you choose:

Data Purpose
Videos you select and the clips produced Creating the clips
Transcripts, analyses and cache Avoiding repeated work
Settings and preferences Remembering your choices
Technical log of the last job (logs) Helping you diagnose errors
Video-platform logins you connect (direct edition only) Downloading videos that require sign-in

Platform logins are stored encrypted with Windows data protection (DPAPI): only your Windows account, on this computer, can open them. They are never sent to us.

You can delete this data at any time: Settings → Output → Clear cache, Disconnect on the accounts, deleting the clips folder, or uninstalling the app.

3. Data we receive

We only receive what you choose to send us, such as a support e-mail or a report about AI-generated content. We then process your e-mail address and the message only to answer and resolve the request (legal basis: performance of contract and legitimate interest, LGPD art. 7, V and IX). We keep these messages as long as needed for support and for up to 5 years for the defense of legal claims.

4. Internet connections and third parties

When With whom What is sent
Purchase, license and updates Microsoft (Microsoft Store) Your Microsoft account and purchase data, handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. The app only asks Windows whether your account holds the license.
Downloading AI models (when you ask) Hugging Face A request for the model file; the server sees your IP address. None of your videos or text is sent.
Online AI or transcription (off by default) OpenAI, Anthropic or another compatible service you configure The video transcript (and, for online transcription, the audio) is sent to the chosen service with your API key, under that service's privacy policy.
Ollama (optional) The Ollama server you specify (usually on your PC) The transcript, so the AI can pick the clips.
Link download (direct edition only) YouTube, Twitch, Kick and GitHub (tools) The video address and, if you connected an account, that account's cookies, sent only to the platform itself.

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

5. Your rights (LGPD, art. 18)

You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent. Since almost all data stays on your computer, you can access and delete it yourself. For data you sent us by e-mail, write to [SUPPORT E-MAIL]. You may also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

6. Security

We use Windows encryption for saved logins, HTTPS connections, integrity checks (SHA-256) of components and, in the Microsoft Store edition, packages signed by Microsoft. No system is 100% secure: keep Windows and your antivirus up to date.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect children's data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is available in the app (Settings → About) and on the app's Microsoft Store page. Relevant changes will be announced in the app.
